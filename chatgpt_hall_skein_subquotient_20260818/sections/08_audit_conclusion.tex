\section{Proof audit, literature boundary and conclusion}
\label{sec:audit}

Because the argument combines exact Hall algebras, surface extensions, skein
algebras and quantum theta bases, it is important to separate source results,
imported theorems, direct proofs and computation.

\subsection{What comes from the supplied morphism-category source}

Li proves the classification framework for indecomposable objects in
$\Mor(\proj A)$, the correspondence between support $\tau$-tilting pairs and
maximal rigid morphism objects, completion of rigid objects, the $2|A|$ count
including contractibles, and the exchange-graph isomorphism \cite{Li}.  These
are inputs.  The supplied article does not prove the homology normal form, the
term-lattice Hall cocycle, the Hall--skein ideal or the global subquotient
theorem.

\subsection{Imported structural results}

We use the following results with citation.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item Bautista supplies structural properties of the projective morphism
      category \cite{Bautista}.
\item The polygon model for type-$A$ cluster categories and the
      support-$\tau$-tilting/two-term silting bridge supply the rigid
      combinatorics \cite{BMRRT,AIR}.
\item Canakci--Schroll supply the extension--crossing and middle-term
      smoothing description for gentle Jacobian algebras of unpunctured
      surfaces \cite{CanakciSchroll}.
\item Muller supplies the multicurve basis and the equality of localized skein
      and quantum cluster algebras for marked surfaces \cite{Muller}.
      Ishibashi--Kano--Yuasa supply geometric coefficient systems by walled
      skein algebras \cite{IshibashiKanoYuasa}; Cao--Huang--Wang supply the
      stated polygon equality and theta-basis refinements
      \cite{CaoHuangWang}.
\item Davison--Mandel identify chamber theta functions with normalized quantum
      cluster monomials \cite{DavisonMandel}.
\item Caldero--Keller, Palu, Chen--Xiao--Xu and Xiao--Xu--Yang supply the
      classical, quantum weighted and motivic $2$-Calabi--Yau multiplication
      background \cite{CalderoKeller,PaluMultiplication,ChenXiaoXu,XiaoXuYang}.
\item Ding--Xu--Zhang and Fu--Peng--Zhang supply the acyclic morphism-Hall
      subquotient model used for comparison
      \cite{DingXuZhang,FuPengZhang}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Direct proofs in this paper}

The following assertions are proved directly after importing the preceding
inputs.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*),leftmargin=2.5em]
\item In type $A$, the centrally reduced exact Hall algebra is generated by
      all diagonal indecomposable classes; hence the source is the entire
      reduced Hall algebra.
\item Non-split exact Hall middle terms are lower than the split term in the
      crossing-smoothing order.
\item Every Hall--skein defect has the split crossing object as its invertible
      leading term.
\item The oriented Hall-corrected crossing reductions terminate.
\item The filtered Diamond argument resolves all overlaps, so non-crossing
      Hall classes form a basis of the quotient.
\item The explicitly generated ideal $\Jsk$ is exactly the kernel of the
      global map to the polygon skein/quantum cluster algebra.
\item Every non-rigid Hall basis object has a unique finite theta
      straightening, and arbitrary incompatible products satisfy the global
      algebra-homomorphism identity.
\end{enumerate}

The conclusion is therefore stronger than the universal kernel criterion of
the preceding paper: in type $A$, the relevant kernel inclusion is proved and
the kernel is generated by the local Hall--skein defects.

\subsection{Finite certificates}

The Python programs use exact symbolic arithmetic.  They enumerate diagonals,
crossing pairs and triangulations, and check the two-smoothing state sum for
all prescribed test multidiagrams.  The $A_4$ program is exhaustive for
ordered triples and degree-four multisets; the cross-rank program provides a
deterministic regression through rank $6$.

These calculations audit endpoint conventions, recursion, confluence and
associativity.  They are not a proof of the arbitrary-$n$ Hall theorem and do
not compute Hall numbers for an arbitrary bound quiver.

\subsection{Literature positioning}

Ding--Xu--Zhang realize an acyclic principal-coefficient quantum cluster
algebra as a subquotient of a morphism Hall algebra, and Fu--Peng--Zhang recover
that realization using a bialgebra and integration map
\cite{DingXuZhang,FuPengZhang}.  Berenstein--Rupel and Chen--Ding--Xu construct
Hall-to-quantum-cluster homomorphisms in hereditary settings
\cite{BerensteinRupel,ChenDingXu}.  These maps benefit from the hereditary Euler
form and do not directly apply to a nonhereditary cluster-tilted algebra.

Caldero--Keller's exceptional Hall algebra realizes finite-type cluster
algebras at the cluster-category level \cite{CalderoKeller}.  Cooper--Samuelson
prove surface skein relations inside derived Hall algebras of partially wrapped
Fukaya categories \cite{CooperSamuelson}.  The present construction is
adjacent to both: its source is the exact projective-morphism Hall algebra of a
cluster-tilted algebra, and its quotient is identified with the polygon skein
algebra.

The targeted literature search used for this revision found no paper stating
the same chain
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\text{exact morphism Hall algebra of a nonhereditary type-$A$ algebra}\\
 &\hspace{2.7em}\longrightarrow
   \text{explicit crossing-defect quotient}\\
 &\hspace{2.7em}\longrightarrow
   \text{polygon quantum cluster algebra}
 \end{aligned}
\]
with a normal-form interpretation of every non-rigid middle term.  This is a
limited search result, not an absolute priority claim.  A formal submission
should receive a wider MathSciNet, zbMATH and citation-chain review.

\subsection{Why the theorem stops at type $A$}

Three ingredients make the proof work.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=2.3em]
\item Every reduced indecomposable object has an arc model and is rigid.
\item Every non-split extension is controlled by local smoothing and strictly
      lowers a crossing order.
\item The target quantum cluster algebra has a local skein presentation with a
      non-crossing/theta basis.
\end{enumerate}
For a general Jacobi-finite cluster-tilted algebra, none of these statements is
available in this form.  Motivic multiplication formulas exist in general
$2$-Calabi--Yau categories, but Xiao--Xu--Yang explicitly note that a general
explicit quantum Laurent formula remains unknown \cite{XiaoXuYang}.  We
therefore do not extend \cref{thm:Hall-skein-subquotient} by analogy alone.

\subsection{Remaining problems}

The present result suggests concrete next steps.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item Extend the Hall--skein quotient to all unpunctured surface
      cluster-tilted/Jacobian algebras, where band objects and essential loops
      must be included.
\item Replace the polygon crossing order by a critical/cohomological Hall
      filtration for arbitrary quivers with potential.
\item Construct a canonical framed critical Hall class attached directly to a
      projective morphism and compare its integration with the quotient map
      $\Phi_{\rm sk}$.
\item Determine whether the Hall-corrected coefficients
      $c_{M,D}(\omega)$ have positivity, bar-invariance or Lefschetz
      properties after a more geometric normalization.
\item Build software which computes the exact Hall coefficients in
      \eqref{eq:Hall-product-containing-M} from a bound quiver and then runs
      the full Hall-to-theta straightening algorithm.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The obstacle left by the previous paper was not the absence of quantum cluster
labels: every rigid morphism already had one.  It was the failure of the rigid
union to be closed under Hall multiplication.  Incompatible products produce
non-rigid middle terms, and the ordinary exact integration does not explain
their Laurent coefficients.

For cluster-tilted type $A$, the polygon supplies the missing global relation.
The exact Hall product is triangular with respect to crossings.  Imposing the
two polygon smoothings as explicit Hall relations turns this triangularity
into a terminating and confluent normal-form system.  Its standard objects are
precisely the rigid non-crossing direct sums.  The polygon skein theorem then
identifies the quotient with the entire frozen-localized quantum cluster
algebra:
\[
 \Hred/\Jsk\cong\Aq.
\]

Thus the non-rigid Hall basis elements do not disappear.  Each receives a
canonical finite expansion in rigid theta functions, and all incompatible
Hall products become the corresponding products in the quantum cluster
algebra.  This closes, in finite type $A$, the final algebraic layer between
the exact morphism Hall algebra and a Ding--Xu--Zhang/Fu--Peng--Zhang style
global subquotient realization.
