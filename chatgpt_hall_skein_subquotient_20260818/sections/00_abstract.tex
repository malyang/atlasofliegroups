\begin{abstract}
Let $A$ be a cluster-tilted algebra of Dynkin type $A_n$ over a finite
field and let
\[
 \MM=\Mor(\proj A)
\]
be the exact category of morphisms between finitely generated projective
right $A$-modules.  Li's maximal-rigid/support-$\tau$-tilting correspondence
puts the rigid part of $\MM$ on the exchange graph, while the preceding
iterations of this project showed that full morphism homology reconstructs
rigid labels, quantum seeds and theta functions.  After a term-lattice
bicharacter correction, Hall multiplication already agrees with quantum
cluster multiplication on every compatibility cone.  The unresolved part was
global: Hall products of incompatible rigid objects contain non-split
extensions and non-rigid middle terms, so the union of the chamber algebras is
not a Hall subalgebra.

For type $A$ we close this final layer by using the polygon model.  Fix the
convex $(n+3)$-gon $P$, a triangulation corresponding to the initial
cluster-tilting object, and a Hall-compatible quantum coefficient system.  We
localize the exact Hall algebra at contractible classes, apply the global
bicharacter correction, and centrally specialize the contractible directions
to the frozen boundary monomials.  The resulting algebra is denoted
$\Hred$.  Its indecomposable reduced basis elements are naturally indexed by
the diagonals of $P$, and because $A$ is representation-finite they generate
the whole centrally reduced Hall algebra, not merely a rigid composition
subalgebra.

For every ordered pair of crossing diagonals $\gamma,\delta$, let
$\gamma\#_0\delta$ and $\gamma\#_\infty\delta$ be the two smoothings.  We
define the Hall--skein defect
\[
 \mathcal R_{\gamma,\delta}
 =\eps_\gamma\star\eps_\delta
  -\omega^{a(\gamma,\delta)}\eps_{\gamma\#_0\delta}
  -\omega^{b(\gamma,\delta)}\eps_{\gamma\#_\infty\delta},
\]
where boundary arcs are interpreted as invertible frozen monomials and the
exponents are those of the chosen walled/stated skein quantization.  Let
$\Jsk$ be the two-sided ideal generated by all such defects.  The main theorem
is the subquotient realization
\[
 \boxed{
  \Hred/\Jsk\;\cong\;
  \Skein_\omega^{\rm fr}(P)
  \;\cong\;
  \mathcal A_\omega^{\rm fr}(P).
 }
\]
Thus, after one localization, one central specialization and one explicit
skein ideal, the \emph{entire} exact morphism Hall algebra maps
surjectively onto the entire frozen-localized quantum cluster algebra.  In the
full-rank coefficient-free regime the same construction specializes to the
global cocycle of the previous paper.  For arbitrary rank it uses the
Hall-compatible principal/walled quantization; coefficient-free and other
geometric coefficients are obtained by the corresponding frozen
specializations.

The proof is triangular rather than tautological.  Canakci--Schroll's
extension--smoothing description implies that a non-split extension of type
$A$ arcs strictly lowers the crossing filtration, whereas the split middle
term is the unique top term of an ordered Hall product.  Hence every
Hall--skein defect has the crossing direct sum as its invertible leading term.
A filtered Diamond-lemma argument, together with the polygon skein basis
theorem, proves that all overlap ambiguities resolve and that the quotient has
a basis indexed by non-crossing multidiagonals.  These are exactly the rigid
direct sums, hence exactly the quantum cluster monomials/theta functions in
cluster chambers.  The equality between the localized polygon skein algebra
and the quantum cluster algebra is supplied by the surface skein--cluster
theorems of Muller and their coefficient/stated refinements.

The construction gives a canonical interpretation of every non-rigid Hall
middle term.  If $M$ is any reduced Hall basis object, recursively solve the
leading Hall product at one crossing and continue until no crossing remains.
The result is independent of all choices and yields a finite expansion
\[
 \operatorname{St}_{\mathrm H}(M)
 =\sum_R c_{M,R}(\omega)\,\vartheta_{g(R)},
\]
where $R$ runs over non-crossing, hence rigid, multidiagonals.  Consequently,
for arbitrary, possibly incompatible, Hall elements $x,y$,
\[
 \Phi_{\rm sk}(x\star y)=\Phi_{\rm sk}(x)\Phi_{\rm sk}(y).
\]
This supplies the missing quantum/theta meaning of all non-rigid extension
strata in finite type $A$.

Deterministic code verifies the polygon counts and the classical Ptolemy
specialization of two-smoothing confluence and associativity for type $A_4$
and across ranks $2$ through $6$.  The quantum certificate requires the full
endpoint-state and wall data and is supplied by the stated/walled skein
theorems used in the proof, not by a bare chord program.  The theorem is
deliberately not extended to arbitrary Jacobi-finite cluster-tilted algebras:
outside the polygon/surface setting, no comparably explicit skein presentation
for all non-rigid objects is currently available.
\end{abstract}
