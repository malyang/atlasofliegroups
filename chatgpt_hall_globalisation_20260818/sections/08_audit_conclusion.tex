\section{Proof audit, literature boundary and conclusion}
\label{sec:audit}

This section separates source results, imported theorems, new proofs and
finite certificates.  The distinction is essential because the paper combines
exact categories, $\tau$-tilting theory, quantum cluster algebras and Hall
integration.

\subsection{What is taken from the source morphism-category paper}

Li proves the indecomposable-object framework in
$\Mor(\proj A)$, the correspondence between maximal rigid morphism objects and
support $\tau$-tilting pairs, completion of rigid objects, the
$2|A|$ count for basic maximal rigid objects including contractibles, and the
exchange-graph isomorphism \cite{Li}.  These are inputs.  The source paper does
not contain the homology normal form, the exact term-lattice Euler form, the
presentation-to-cluster fan transport, the quantum theta reconstruction or the
globalization and holonomy results proved here.

\subsection{Imported structural theorems}

The following ingredients are used with citation.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item AIR supplies the support $\tau$-tilting/two-term silting
      correspondence and completion of presilting objects \cite{AIR}.
\item Demonet--Iyama--Jasso supply unimodular two-term silting cones and
      completeness in the $\tau$-tilting finite case \cite{DIJ}.
\item Derksen--Fei supply presentation spaces, generic $E$-invariants and
      canonical decompositions \cite{DerksenFei}.
\item Palu and Plamondon supply the classical cluster-character and generic-
      character framework \cite{Palu,PlamondonGeneric}.
\item Nakanishi--Zelevinsky supply tropical duality for $G$- and $C$-matrices
      \cite{NakanishiZelevinsky}.
\item Berenstein--Zelevinsky supply quantum seed mutation
      \cite{BZ}; Gellert--Lampe supply the general quantisation-space
      perspective \cite{GellertLampe}.
\item Davison--Mandel identify a quantum theta function in a cluster chamber
      with the normalized quantum cluster monomial and prove positive theta
      multiplication \cite{DavisonMandel}.
\item Zhang supplies the standard exact Hall integration used before the
      bicharacter twist \cite{ZhangFixedSize}.
\item Keller--Yang, Bridgeland and Davison supply the mutation-covariant
      $3$-Calabi--Yau and cohomological wall-crossing background
      \cite{KellerYang,BridgelandScattering,Davison}.
\end{enumerate}

Recent work describes the $\tau$-cluster morphism category directly from the
wall-and-chamber geometry of an algebra
\cite{SchrollTattarTreffingerWilliams}.  This supports the geometric relevance
of the transfer group, but it does not state the Hall-cocycle globalization
criterion of \cref{thm:globalization-criterion}.

\subsection{Direct proofs in this paper}

The following assertions are proved directly here.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\Alph*),leftmargin=2.5em]
\item A contractible-radical term form with initial boundary parameter $S$ is
      uniquely $D^TSD$.
\item Its restrictions reproduce all mutable quantum chamber forms if and
      only if $S$ is fixed by every presentation-to-cluster transfer.
\item The globalizable parameters form the holonomy invariant lattice
      $\Qglob$ and are computed by a homogeneous integer linear system.
\item The canonical principal parameter $S=0$ gives one global mutable Hall
      cocycle in arbitrary rank.
\item Adjacent relative transfers are unipotent rank-one shears
      $I+v\ell^T$ with $\ell^Tv=0$.
\item Such a shear preserves $S$ exactly when $Sv$ is an integral multiple of
      the primitive wall covector.
\item Full-rank compatible transfers are integral symplectic and their
      nonzero walls are symplectic transvections.
\item A full fixed-frozen principal globalization exists exactly when the
      mutable invariance and the mixed equation
      $(\Delta_R^T+G_R^T)(dI+SB_0)=0$ both hold.
\item The canonical $S=0$ full fixed-frozen no-go and the full-rank
      $S=\Lambda_0$ recovery follow as exact corollaries.
\item The universal rigid-face colimit admits a global Hall-to-theta quotient
      exactly when $\ker\pi_{\mathrm H}\subseteq\ker\pi_\vartheta$.
\item Initial normalization plus one-step mutation covariance uniquely
      recognizes the integrated critical assignment on every reachable rigid
      morphism.
\end{enumerate}

The earlier homology normal form, orbit-normal-space theorem, fan--orbit
equivalence and local Hall--theta chamber isomorphism are retained as proved
inputs from the previous unified iteration; they are summarized in
\cref{sec:setup} so the new arguments are self-contained at the level of
lattice and Hall multiplication.

\subsection{Finite certificates}

The two new Python verifiers are deterministic.  The $A_4$ verifier computes
the complete finite paired atlas, the invariant skew lattice, all wall
factorizations, and the full fixed-frozen parameter space.  The cross-rank
verifier samples mutation prefixes for four full-rank matrices and two
singular matrices.  Both use SymPy exact arithmetic and stop on the first
failed assertion.

A finite certificate is not a proof of an arbitrary-rank theorem.  Its role is
to audit signs, transpose conventions, rank-one factors, affine constants and
implementation consistency.  The arbitrary-rank statements are proved before
the example.

\subsection{Literature positioning}

Direct Hall realizations of quantum cluster algebras from morphism categories
are established in acyclic hereditary settings
\cite{DingXuZhang,FuPengZhang}.  Chen--Hu's 2026 quantum Weyl relations for
two-term complexes also assume a Dynkin path algebra \cite{ChenHu}.  Contu's
semi-derived Hall quantum cluster structure concerns bounded projective
complexes over a representation-finite path algebra \cite{Contu}.  The present
scope is different: the exact morphism category may come from a
nonhereditary cluster-tilted algebra, and the result is a globalizable
bicharacter criterion with exact chamber algebra charts, not a global quantum
cluster algebra structure on all Hall basis elements.

Gellert--Lampe classify quantisation spaces under full-rank hypotheses
\cite{GellertLampe}.  The invariant lattice $\Qglob$ is not offered as a
replacement for that theory.  It solves a different comparison problem: which
principal quantisation parameters can be pulled back through every
presentation-to-cluster fan chart to one exact Hall term form.  The answer is
the fixed lattice of the transfer group.

The literature search used for this draft found no source combining the
following four statements in one theorem:
\[
 \text{holonomy-fixed quantisation parameters},
 \quad
 \text{rank-one symplectic wall criterion},
 \quad
 \text{fixed-frozen mixed obstruction},
 \quad
 \text{Hall/theta kernel factorization}.
\]
This is a limited search result, not an absolute priority claim.  A formal
submission should undergo a wider MathSciNet, zbMATH and citation-chain review.

\subsection{Remaining questions}

The preceding iterations and the present paper leave the following concrete
problems.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item Prove the kernel inclusion
      $\ker\pi_{\mathrm H}\subseteq\ker\pi_\vartheta$, or construct an
      explicit counterexample.  This is the exact algebraic form of the
      incompatible-product problem.
\item Construct a multiplicative filtration or completion in the direction of
      \cref{conj:codimension-filtration}, and identify its positive-degree
      pieces with critical or cohomological Hall strata.
\item Construct a canonical framed critical Hall/Donaldson--Thomas class
      $\Xi_T(f)$ for each reachable rigid projective morphism.  By
      \cref{cor:critical-reduction}, only initial normalization and mutation
      covariance remain to identify its integration.
\item Determine $\Qglob$ and $\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{ff}}$ for broad infinite
      families, and relate the transfer group to cluster modular groups and
      scattering fan monodromy.
\item Understand whether non-complete $\tau$-tilting fans necessarily have
      rational or integral weights outside their support.  The real
      incompleteness statement alone does not imply this arithmetic conclusion.
\item Build general software which derives minimal presentations, second
      syzygies, support-$\tau$-tilting charts and map-orbit strata directly
      from an arbitrary bound quiver.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The exact Hall term lattice and the cluster lattice carry different
information.  The term lattice retains the projective source and target but
forgets the differential after canonical integration.  Rigid-open-orbit
geometry recovers the unique differential on the rigid locus.  The split
index then locates a presentation-fan point, and the piecewise map
$\Gamma_T$ supplies the cluster $g$-vector and theta label.

The present paper identifies the precise extra datum needed for
multiplication.  A principal quantisation parameter $S$ is compatible with
one global exact Hall cocycle if and only if
\[
 \mathsf L_R^TS\mathsf L_R=S
\]
for every rigid chamber.  The global term form is then forced to be
$D^TSD$, and the cocycle-corrected Hall multiplication agrees with quantum
theta multiplication on every compatibility cone.  The zero parameter always
works, so singular rank is not an obstruction to a global canonical mutable
cocycle.  Full rank supplies a nondegenerate invariant form and makes the
fan transport piecewise symplectic.

The frozen directions reveal a genuinely stronger obstruction.  A fixed
principal frozen lattice exists exactly when
\[
 (\Delta_R^T+G_R^T)(dI+SB_0)=0
\]
in addition to mutable invariance.  The canonical parameter usually fails
this test, while the full-rank coefficient-free parameter makes the frozen
block central and succeeds.  This explains why principal quantum seeds can be
transported globally even when no one fixed-frozen Hall term form realizes all
of them.

Finally, the universal face colimit isolates incompatible products as the
kernel inclusion
\[
 \ker\pi_{\mathrm H}\subseteq\ker\pi_\vartheta.
\]
The exact Hall algebra and the quantum theta algebra are therefore no longer
separated by an unspecified ``missing map'': the missing theorem is an
explicit comparison of two relation ideals, together with a geometric
construction of the critical class whose integrated value is already forced
by mutation recognition.
