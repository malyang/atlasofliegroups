\begin{abstract}
Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional algebra and
$\MM=\Mor(\proj A)$ the exact category of morphisms between finitely generated
projective right $A$-modules.  Li's maximal-rigid/support-$\tau$-tilting
correspondence places the rigid part of $\MM$ on the two-term silting fan.  In a
cluster-tilted realization, earlier stages of this project proved that full
morphism homology reconstructs the reduced object, its extension spaces,
cluster character, quantum seed and quantum theta label.  They also constructed,
when the mutable exchange matrix is invertible, one global bicharacter twist of
the exact Hall algebra whose multiplication agrees with quantum cluster
multiplication on every rigid compatibility cone.  The singular-rank case was
left only chamberwise.

This paper closes that gap and identifies the exact obstruction.  Let $K$ be
the split projective lattice, $L=K\oplus K$ the exact term lattice,
$D=(-I\ I):L\to K$ the split-difference map, and let
$R=f_1\oplus\cdots\oplus f_n$ be a reachable ordered basic maximal rigid
morphism object.  Write $\Delta_R$ for its split-index matrix, $G_R$ for the
cluster $g$-matrix reconstructed from homology, and
\[
 \mathsf L_R=G_R\Delta_R^{-1}
\]
for the chamber transfer from presentation weights to cluster $g$-vectors.
For a skew form $S$ on $K$, the affine principal quantisation transported to
$R$ has mutable block $G_R^TSG_R$.  We prove that a single
contractible-radical term-lattice form with initial boundary value $S$ exists
if and only if
\[
 \Delta_R^TS\Delta_R=G_R^TSG_R
 \quad\text{for every reachable }R,
\]
equivalently
\[
 \mathsf L_R^TS\mathsf L_R=S.
\]
Thus all globalizable Hall quantisations form the integral fixed lattice
\[
 \Qglob=\Skew(K)^{\Hol},
\]
where $\Hol$ is the presentation-fan transfer group.  The global target form is
necessarily $D^TSD$, and the correcting Hall cocycle is
$D^TSD-\Lambda_{\MM}$.  In particular $S=0$ always globalizes, with no rank
assumption.  Therefore the canonical principal-coefficient quantum atlas has
one global exact-Hall cocycle in every skew-symmetric rank; on each rigid
chamber its mutable cluster variables commute, exactly as prescribed by the
canonical principal quantum form.  When $B_0^T\Lambda_0=dI$, the form
$S=\Lambda_0$ is fixed by the whole transfer group and recovers the earlier
coefficient-free theorem.

Adjacent chamber transfers differ by rank one.  We prove that the relative
transition has the form
\[
 T=I+v\ell^T,
 \qquad \ell^Tv=0,
\]
where $\ell$ is the primitive integral wall covector.  For any integral skew
form $S$,
\[
 T^TST=S
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 Sv=c\ell\quad\text{for some }c\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Consequently every nontrivial wall in the full-rank atlas is an integral
symplectic transvection.  This turns the piecewise-unimodular presentation-to-
cluster map into a piecewise symplectic map and gives an edge-local algorithm
for computing $\Qglob$.

The full principal seed carries frozen directions, and here a second
obstruction appears.  Requiring one global form on $L\oplus K_{\mathrm{fr}}$
with a fixed frozen basis and all contractibles in its radical is equivalent to
the mutable invariance above together with
\[
 (\Delta_R^T+G_R^T)(dI+SB_0)=0
 \quad\text{for every }R.
\]
Hence the canonical choice $S=0$ globalizes mutably but, unless
$\Delta_R=-G_R$ in every chamber, cannot globalize the full fixed-frozen
principal form.  By contrast, in the full-rank scalar-compatible case
$S=\Lambda_0$ satisfies $dI+\Lambda_0B_0=0$ and globalizes the whole principal
form, with central frozen block.  Thus the correct arbitrary-rank structure is
a global mutable Hall cocycle together with a generally crossed frozen atlas.

Finally we formulate the incompatible-product problem as a precise kernel
comparison.  The rigid face algebras have a universal colimit
$\mathscr U_T$ with canonical maps both to the cocycle-twisted exact Hall
algebra and to the reachable quantum theta algebra.  A global Hall-to-theta
homomorphism extending all chamber maps exists exactly when the Hall kernel is
contained in the theta kernel.  This criterion isolates the remaining
non-rigid extension problem without discarding any Hall strata.  We also prove
a recognition principle: any future framed critical Hall assignment with the
correct initial normalization and mutation covariance is forced, on every
reachable rigid morphism, to integrate to
$\vartheta_{\Gamma_T(I(f))}$.

For the nonhereditary cluster-tilted $A_4$ algebra, deterministic exact code
enumerates all \AIVLabeledStates\ labelled paired seeds and \AIVChambers\
rigid chambers, computes the invariant skew lattice, checks all
\AIVOrientedMutations\ oriented walls, and solves the complete fixed-frozen
parameter system.  It verifies the global canonical mutable cocycle, the
symplectic-transvection identities, the fixed-frozen obstruction and the
unique full global parameter.  Cross-rank regressions cover full-rank examples
in ranks $2,4,6$ and singular path matrices in ranks $3,5$.  No global
homomorphism on incompatible Hall products and no direct exact-to-critical
Hall comparison is claimed.
\end{abstract}
